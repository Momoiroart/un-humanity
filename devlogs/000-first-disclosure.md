The world looks normal. It isn't. This is the first entry in the file:
what UN-HUMANITY is, and where development starts.

## What this is

**UN-HUMANITY** is a third-person action-investigation about the people who
can see the cracks in reality — and the covert organization that pays them
to keep everyone else comfortable.

**The loop:** Tip → Investigate → Classify → Engage → Resolve → go home and
act normal. Not everything you find is hostile. Wrong calls have consequences
either way.

**The rule:** human power costs what the craft costs. A writer's power needs
believers; a painter's power needs skill. Un-Humanity is every power that
skips the price — and that's exactly what makes it monstrous.
